Material is listed in crafting.cs for basic AC bonus per part.
From type to type Chainmail is heavier and less protective than Plate, but more protective than Leather. Leather is the lightest and least protective.

Example:
Bronze Plate Helmet 8 AC bonus, 2kg , 350 copper
Bronze Chainmail Coif 7 AC bonus, 2.2kg, 200 copper

We will add more examples here as we create them.
